# Sketch2Shape AI — Geometry Intelligence & Asset Refinement Architecture

## 1. System Overview

Stage 9 implements an advanced, mathematically rigorous **3D Geometry Intelligence and Automated Refinement Engine** under `backend/app/geometry_intelligence/`. The engine provides automated quality auditing, defect detection, multi-view consistency evaluation, and volume-preserving refinement across all 3D assets generated by the platform.

## 2. Module Specifications

### 2.1 Topology Analyzer ([`topology_analyzer.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/topology_analyzer.py))
* **Vertex, Face, and Unique Edge Counts:** Evaluates fundamental mesh elements.
* **Watertightness & Closed Volume:** Computes boundary edges ($E_{boundary}$, edges with only 1 adjacent face). Watertight manifolds have $E_{boundary} = 0$.
* **Euler Characteristic ($\chi$):** Computed as:
  $$\chi = V - E + F = 2(1 - g)$$
  where genus $g=0$ for a spherical closed manifold ($\chi = 2$).
* **Non-Manifold Edges:** Counts edges shared by $>2$ adjacent faces.
* **Degenerate Faces:** Identifies zero-area, colinear, or duplicate-vertex triangles.
* **Redundant Vertices:** Counts duplicate coincident vertices within distance tolerance $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$.

### 2.2 Surface Analyzer ([`surface_analyzer.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/surface_analyzer.py))
* **Surface Area:** Exact summation of triangular facet areas.
* **Mathematical Volume:** Divergence theorem integral over closed watertight manifolds. Open meshes correctly yield `None` with `is_volume_valid = False`.
* **Winding Order & Normal Consistency:** Validates continuous orientation across adjacent triangles and flags inverted face normals.
* **Curvature Approximation:** Discrete Gaussian curvature via angle deficit $\delta(v) = 2\pi - \sum \theta_i$, and mean curvature via discrete Laplace-Beltrami vertex displacements.
* **Surface Roughness:** Standard deviation of Laplacian coordinate residuals.

### 2.3 Spatial & Symmetry Analyzer ([`symmetry_analyzer.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/symmetry_analyzer.py))
* **Bounding-Box Extents:** Minimum, maximum, and $(X, Y, Z)$ dimensions in world space.
* **Aspect Ratios:** Calculates $X/Y$, $Z/Y$, and $X/Z$ ratios to detect pancake or needle distortions.
* **Center of Mass:** Analytical volumetric center of mass for watertight solids, or surface centroid for open shells.
* **Plane Reflection Symmetry:** Measures bilateral symmetry across principal planes ($X=0, Y=0, Z=0$) by reflecting vertices:
  $$V_{refl} = V \cdot \text{diag}(-1, 1, 1)$$
  and calculating KDTree nearest-neighbor distances normalized by bounding diagonal:
  $$\text{Score}_{sym} = \exp\left(-6.0 \cdot \frac{\bar{d}}{\text{diag}}\right) \in [0.0, 1.0]$$

### 2.4 Connected Component Analyzer ([`component_analyzer.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/component_analyzer.py))
* **Sub-Mesh Partitioning:** Segregates disconnected geometry via topological edge connectivity.
* **Isolated Fragment Detection:** Flags sub-meshes possessing $<3\%$ of total model faces.
* **Protrusion & Spike Detection:** Evaluates radial distance distribution $r_i = \|v_i - c\|_2$. Outliers exceeding $Q_{75} + 3.0 \cdot \text{IQR}$ are classified as abnormal geometric spikes.

### 2.5 Multi-View Consistency Inspector ([`multiview_inspector.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/multiview_inspector.py))
* **7 Canonical Camera Perspectives:** Front, Back, Left, Right, Top, Bottom, and Three-Quarter Perspective.
* **Hardware Acceleration:** Employs Open3D's Vulkan Offscreen Renderer running directly on the NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU.
* **Silhouette Occupancy:** Calculates pixel foreground ratio across each viewpoint.
* **Front View Sketch IoU:** Calculates Intersection over Union between the rendered front-facing silhouette and the original 2D input sketch:
  $$\text{IoU} = \frac{|\text{Mask}_{render} \cap \text{Mask}_{sketch}|}{|\text{Mask}_{render} \cup \text{Mask}_{sketch}|}$$
* **Hidden Surface Distinction:** Transparently reports that single-view 2D sketches only constrain visible contours; hidden surface consistency is assessed via cross-view continuity.

### 2.6 Defect Detector ([`defect_detector.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/defect_detector.py))
* Checks for 8 concrete failure modes: non-finite coordinates, non-manifold edges, open boundary holes, degenerate triangles, duplicate vertices, isolated shards, unoriented normals, and extreme spikes.

### 2.7 Automated Mesh Refinement Pipeline ([`mesh_refiner.py`](file:///C:/Sketch2Shape-AI/backend/app/geometry_intelligence/mesh_refiner.py))
* **Volume-Preserving Taubin Smoothing:** Replaces destructive Laplacian smoothing with Taubin's two-step filter alternating positive $\lambda$ and negative $\mu$ steps ($\lambda=0.40, \mu=-0.43, |\mu| > \lambda$). Attenuates high-frequency surface noise **without shrinking object volume**.
* **Quadric Decimation:** Safely simplifies high-polygon meshes (>20,000 faces) to optimal web budgets while strictly avoiding decimation on low-poly structures (<1,000 faces).
* **Shard Pruning:** Prunes floating disconnected shards containing $<3\%$ of faces.
* **Three Refinement Presets:** `FAST`, `BALANCED`, and `HIGH`.

## 3. API Integration Architecture

Endpoints implemented in `backend/app/routes/geometry_v1.py`:
* `POST /api/v1/geometry/analyze`: Complete topological, surface, defect, and multi-view audit.
* `POST /api/v1/geometry/refine`: Safe mesh refinement with FAST, BALANCED, HIGH presets.
* `GET /api/v1/geometry/report/{asset_id}`: Retrieves cached diagnostic report.
* `GET /api/v1/geometry/compare/{asset_id}`: Retrieves before-and-after refinement comparison metrics.
* `GET /api/v1/geometry/download/{asset_id}/{format}`: Streams refined binary GLB or OBJ.
